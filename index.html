01 — WHO WE ARE

A future built
with the people.

AES SOUTH is a registered non-profit company whose constitution sets out a community-centred vision for land, heritage and natural-resource sovereignty across Southern Africa.

Inspired by the Alliance of Sahel States, the Southern Wing seeks to bring communities, traditional custodians, young people and aligned partners into a shared conversation about ownership, local industry and the future of our region.

04 — OUR FRAMEWORK

Community at
the centre.

The constitution describes a proposed 51% sovereign community ownership framework for qualifying projects on communal, traditional or trust land under AES SOUTH's stated jurisdiction.

10%Khoisan First Nation Council
20%Local iNkosi / Ingonyama Trust

Why this matters: the constitution identifies direct benefit for youth, women and the 195 Boshoff families as a purpose of the AES SOUTH Community Trust allocation.

GOVERNANCE

The Grand Council
