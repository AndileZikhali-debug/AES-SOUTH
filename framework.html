04 — OUR FRAMEWORK

Community at
the centre.

The constitution describes a proposed 51% sovereign community ownership framework for qualifying projects on communal, traditional or trust land under AES SOUTH's stated jurisdiction.

10%Khoisan First Nation Council
20%Local iNkosi / Ingonyama Trust
21%AES SOUTH Community Trust
49% maxCommercial technical investor

Why this matters: the constitution identifies direct benefit for youth, women and the 195 Boshoff families as a purpose of the AES SOUTH Community Trust allocation.

GOVERNANCE

The Grand Council

The iNkundla is identified as the supreme decision-making body, alongside the Executive Directorate and Heritage Council.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Transparency matters

The constitution provides for independent annual audits, transaction authorisation controls and annual public disclosure of financial information.

MEMBERSHIP

A shared covenant

Article 5 sets out membership eligibility, and Article 9 contains the membership covenant and pledge.
